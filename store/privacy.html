Ask C4S — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Overview

Ask C4S is a browser extension that assists users of SAP Service Cloud (C4S / C4C). It connects to a backend service configured by your organization’s administrator (the “Service”) to help triage tickets, search the knowledge base, check parts, and draft replies.

What the extension accesses

  • Page content on SAP Service Cloud pages (*.crm.ondemand.com, *.crm.sap.com, *.c4c.sap.com, *.cloudfoundry.io): when you invoke the extension (right-click, slash command, or the on-page button), it may read the current page to detect the ticket you are viewing and provide relevant context.
  • Text you select or type: selections you act on and prompts you enter.
  • Your sign-in session: an authentication token and account details, stored locally in your browser.

What is sent, and where

When you make a request, the extension sends your query and the relevant page/ticket context to the backend Service configured by your administrator (the “Server URL” in settings). That Service:

  • retrieves data from your organization’s SAP Service Cloud tenant to answer your request, and
  • may send the request and context to a third-party AI provider (for example, Anthropic or OpenAI) to generate a response, as configured by your administrator.

The extension itself does not transmit your data to any party other than the configured backend Service.

What is stored locally

Stored in your browser (chrome.storage), on your device only: your authentication token and account info (to keep you signed in), and your settings (e.g., Server URL, display preferences, SLA-watcher settings). These are sent only to the configured Service as described above. You can clear them by signing out or removing the extension.

Data sharing and sale

Ask C4S does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties other than the backend Service you connect to (and, via that Service, the AI provider your administrator configures).

Data retention

Retention at the backend Service, SAP, and any AI provider is governed by your organization’s policies and those providers’ terms. The extension retains only the local items listed above.

Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions needed to perform its function; each is explained in the Chrome Web Store listing.
